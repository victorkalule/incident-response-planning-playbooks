# Publication Review Checklist

Before switching the repository from **private** to **public**:

- [ ] Confirm TripleTen training terms allow an original public-facing summary of the incident response planning assignment.
- [ ] Confirm whether the fictional organization name and case framing may be published.
- [ ] Avoid uploading original course templates, training instructions, or any material marked confidential.
- [ ] Verify that all summarized procedures match the original Capybara Unlimited IRP.
- [ ] Remove any personal data, passwords, private company information, internal contact lists, or nonpublic evidence.
- [ ] Clearly label this as **incident response plan and playbook design** rather than professional incident execution.
- [ ] Do not imply that listed vendor tools were deployed or used just because the training plan mentions them.
- [ ] Do not claim actual detection times, containment rates, completed tabletops, or operational results without corresponding evidence.
- [ ] Review all links, file paths, and document formatting before publication.
