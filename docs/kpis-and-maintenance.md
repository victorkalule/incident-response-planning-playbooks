# Proposed Incident Response KPIs & Maintenance

**Status:** Measures and practices *defined* in the training IRP, not observed operating results.

## Proposed measures

| KPI | Source-defined meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Time to Detect (TTD) | Time from incident start until it is first detected |
| Time to Contain (TTC) | Time from detection until the threat is contained |
| Time to Recover Baseline Security Posture (TTRBSP) | Time from containment to restoration of normal operations and services |
| Incident Closure Rate | Share of incidents resolved and documented within a defined service-level agreement |
| Post-Incident Improvement Rate | Share of lessons-learned actions actually implemented |

The source does **not** provide measured KPI values, baselines, agreed SLAs, or targets. These metrics should therefore remain **proposed**.

## Common pitfalls identified in the source plan

- **Overlooking early indicators:** Correlate weaker signals instead of dismissing them prematurely; improve alert prioritization.
- **Incomplete documentation:** Keep a centralized response record and designate a scribe for more significant incidents.
- **Premature service restoration:** Verify eradication and gain authorized approval before reconnecting affected systems.
- **Skipping lessons learned:** Perform post-incident reviews for significant events and track resulting changes to completion.

## Review and exercise cadence

The training IRP recommends:
- Reviewing the plan **at least annually** and after major incidents or changes to systems, business operations, or regulations.
- Conducting **quarterly or semiannual** response training and tabletop exercises.
- Using audits and follow-up tracking to drive continuous improvement.

These are recommended practices in the source document; there is no evidence in the provided plan that exercises or audits were already completed.
